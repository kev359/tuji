## 💡 Design Tips for Content

### **When Adding New Features:**
1. Use gradient backgrounds for cards
2. Apply consistent shadows (lg)
3. Use white text on gradient backgrounds
4. Add hover effects to interactive elements
5. Maintain 300ms transition timing

### **Color Usage Guide:**
- **Success/Confirmed:** Teal gradient
- **Warning/Pending:** Orange/Amber
- **Error/Rejected:** Red
- **Info/Active:** Purple gradient
- **Neutral/Completed:** Gray

### **Spacing:**
- Cards: `p-6` (24px padding)
- Buttons: `px-6 py-3` (24px × 12px)
- Icons: `w-7 h-7` (28px × 28px)
- Gaps: `gap-6` (24px between items)
